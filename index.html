About Green Sparks

Green Sparks is a community-powered environmental campaign born on fca students group

From dandora to Nairobi's rivers and the Rift Valley's forests, we want to partner with schools, county governments, and local leaders to turn environmental awareness into measurable action.

What Drives Us

Mission, Vision & Core Values

Our Mission

To mobilize Kenyan communities to reduce plastic pollution, restore green cover, and build climate resilience through hands-on action and environmental education.

Our Vision

A Kenya where every community, river, and forest thrives free of plastic waste — sustained by communities who see themselves as its guardians.

Community First

Local people leading local solutions.

Integrity

Transparent about our impact and funding.

Sustainability

Solutions built to outlast a single campaign.

Education

Knowledge that turns awareness into action.
