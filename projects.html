Nairobi River Projects

Restoring the Nairobi River Together

Green Sparks works with communities, schools, volunteers and partners to restore the Nairobi River through clean-up campaigns, tree planting and environmental education.

River Clean-Up

Nairobi River Community Clean-Up

Green Sparks organizes monthly clean-up drives along the Nairobi River, bringing together volunteers, schools, and community groups to remove plastic waste, improve water quality, and restore the river's natural beauty.

80% of this year's clean-up target completed •

Waste Management

Plastic Recycling Initiative

Collected plastic waste from the Nairobi River is sorted and delivered to recycling partners, reducing pollution while creating income opportunities for local youth groups.

65 tonnes of plastic diverted from the river • Since 2026

River Restoration

Riparian Tree Planting Program

To restore river ecosystems and reduce soil erosion, Green Sparks plants indigenous trees along the banks of the Nairobi River in partnership with schools, local residents, and environmental organizations.
