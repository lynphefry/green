Our Story

From one River clean-up to a national movement

Green Sparks began in 2026 when a small group of friends in JERICHO organized a Saturday clean-up along korogocho river. What they found — tonnes of single-use plastic thrown in the river.

Today, Green Sparks operates in nairobi, running recycling programs, reforestation drives, and climate education for schools and communities. who believe Kenya's environment is worth protecting.

We're not just cleaning up — we're building the systems, habits, and next generation of leaders who will keep it that way.

Mission

To mobilize Kenyan communities to reduce plastic pollution, restore green cover, and build climate resilience through hands-on action, transparent partnerships, and environmental education that reaches every county.

Vision

A Kenya where every coastline, river, and forest thrives free of plastic waste — sustained by communities who see themselves as its guardians, and where climate action is a shared, everyday practice.

Our Core Values
